# Responsible use

- Synthetic data may not represent real populations, lending products or outcomes.
- No real customer data is required or included; applicant submissions are not persisted or logged as payloads.
- Predictions are uncertain, uncalibrated and for demonstration only.
- Disparate performance and bias have not been assessed. A future audit needs lawful, carefully governed data and qualified review; protected traits are not model inputs.
- Model explanations are not causal conclusions. SHAP is currently unavailable in the UI/API.
- No automatic approval or rejection is implemented. Real use would require independent validation, fairness and calibration analysis, governance, regulatory review and human oversight.
